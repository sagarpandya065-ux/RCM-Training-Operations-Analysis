# RCM Training Operations Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes **RCM (Revenue Cycle Management) training operations** using **Excel, SQL, and Power BI**.

The objective is to understand training performance, identify operational trends, and generate business insights that can help training and operations teams monitor associate performance and training effectiveness.

The dataset contains **1,200 training records** covering different training types, experience levels, RCM functions, assessment scores, quality scores, productivity, planned vs. actual training days, and trainer utilization.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Excel** – Data exploration, cleaning, pivot tables, and initial analysis
* **MySQL** – Data querying and analytical calculations
* **Power BI** – Interactive dashboard and visualization
* **DAX** – KPI calculations and performance measures

## 📌 Key Areas Analyzed

* Training performance
* Assessment and quality scores
* First Pass Ratio
* Planned vs. Actual training days
* Extended training
* Productivity
* Retraining hours
* Floor support
* Trainer utilization
* Experience-level comparison
* Training-type comparison
* RCM function performance

## 📈 Key KPIs

* **Total Training Records:** 1,200
* **First Pass Ratio:** 79%
* **Average Assessment Score:** 81.29
* **Average Quality Score:** 85.86
* **Average Productivity Score:** 82.27
* **Training Extension Rate:** 8%
* **Average Planned Training Days:** 9.01
* **Average Actual Training Days:** 8.99

## 🔍 Business Insights

The analysis provides visibility into how different training types and experience levels perform across key operational metrics.

It helps identify:

* Areas where training requires additional time
* Differences between Fresher and Experienced associates
* Training types with stronger or weaker performance indicators
* Relationships between assessment, quality, and productivity
* Trainer utilization and operational capacity
* Opportunities to improve training effectiveness and reduce unnecessary extensions
